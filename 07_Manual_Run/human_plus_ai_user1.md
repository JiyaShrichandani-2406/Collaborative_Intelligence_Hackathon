# Human + AI Run — User 1

## Real Ticket

**Issue:** Application login failing after password reset.

The client successfully reset the password but still cannot log in and gets the message: **“Unable to authenticate user. Please try again.”**

They already tried the new password, another browser, and clearing cache and cookies.

## AI Response

The AI created a professional response, asked for the username/email, application URL, login time, and screenshot, and suggested a few additional troubleshooting steps.

## Human Review

I compared the AI response with the ticket and the human response.

I found that the AI made a few assumptions, such as saying **“we will review your account and authentication logs”** and **“we will prioritize the investigation.”** The ticket does not confirm that we have access to these logs or that the issue has been prioritized.

The human response also claimed that the issue had already been investigated and resolved, which was not supported by the ticket.

So I removed these unsupported claims and kept only the information that was actually available.

## Final Human + AI Response

Hello,

Thank you for providing the details.

We understand that your password reset was successful, but you are still unable to log in and receive the message: “Unable to authenticate user. Please try again.”

Since you have already tried your new password, another browser, and clearing your cache and cookies, please share:

* Your username or registered email
* Application URL
* Approximate time of the login attempt
* Screenshot of the error, if possible

Once we receive these details, we can review the information and advise you on the next steps.

Kind regards,
Support Team

## Human Contribution

AI prepared the first draft. I checked it against the actual ticket, removed unsupported claims, and made the final response safe and appropriate for the client.
