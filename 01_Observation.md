STEP 1 : OBSERVATION

Technical support professionals receive client queries through communication channels such as email or support systems.
They first understand the issue, identify the type of query, check the available information or technical documentation,
determine whether additional information is required, and prepare a response for the client. Some queries can be handled 
quickly, while others take more time because the issue is unclear, information is missing, documentation needs to be checked,
or the response requires technical judgement or approval from another person. Before communicating with the client, 
the support professional needs to ensure that the response is correct, relevant to the issue, and appropriate to send.

WORKFLOW OF OBSERVATION:
Client query → Understand → Check information → Prepare response → Verify → Communicate
