# STEP 3: HYPOTHESIS

## Hypothesis

A collaborative workflow in which a plain LLM prepares an initial client response and a technical support employee reviews, verifies, and edits the response before sending can reduce the time required to prepare a client-ready response compared with the employee working alone, while maintaining the accuracy and relevance of the response through human verification.

## Target

The human + AI workflow should:

* Reduce response-preparation time from the current baseline of **5 minutes per support query**.
* Maintain a response that is **accurate, relevant to the client's issue, and safe to communicate**.
* Keep the final decision and approval with the technical support employee.

## Kill Condition

The hypothesis will be considered unsupported if the human + AI workflow does not reduce the response-preparation time below **6-10 minutes**, or if the AI-generated draft contains important inaccuracies, missing information, or inappropriate statements that require substantial correction by the employee.

## What We Will Compare

Human alone
→ Time + accuracy

Plain LLM
→ Time + accuracy

Human + AI
→ Time + accuracy after human verification
